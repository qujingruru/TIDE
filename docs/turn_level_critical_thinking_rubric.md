# Turn-Level Critical Thinking Scoring Rubric for Dialogue

## Purpose and provenance

This study rubric adapts the Critical Thinking Analytic Rubric (CTAR; Saxton,
Belanger, & Becker, 2012) to turn-level argumentative dialogue. It retains
three dimensions from the six-dimension CTAR: Analysis, Evaluation, and
Inference. The third dimension is labelled **Reasoning** in the study data.
The underlying CTAR remains attributed to its original authors; this document
records the adaptation used in the study for research transparency.

Each target student turn is scored from 1 to 6 on all three dimensions. Raters
may read the surrounding dialogue to identify the points of view, evidence,
counterarguments, concepts, and implications addressed by the target turn.

## Analysis

Analysis concerns the analysis of points of view and the identification of
claims, arguments, patterns, and assumptions in the evidence.

| Score | Descriptor |
|---:|---|
| 6 | Thoughtfully analyzes all points of view to present a thorough evaluation of similarities and differences; accurately identifies important claims, arguments, and patterns in the evidence. |
| 5 | Analyzes all points of view to present a thorough evaluation of similarities and differences; accurately identifies claims, arguments, patterns, and/or assumptions in the evidence. |
| 4 | Analyzes all points of view to present an evaluation of similarities and differences; identifies claims, arguments, patterns, and/or assumptions in the evidence. |
| 3 | Analyzes all points of view to present an evaluation of obvious or oversimplified similarities and differences; superficially identifies the basic claims and arguments in the evidence. |
| 2 | Presents a superficial analysis of similarities and differences between the various points of view; incorrectly identifies claims, arguments, and/or assumptions in the evidence. |
| 1 | Presents little to no analysis of similarities and differences; does not identify claims, arguments, and/or assumptions in the evidence. |

## Evaluation

Evaluation concerns the evaluation of arguments from multiple perspectives.

| Score | Descriptor |
|---:|---|
| 6 | Identifies the salient arguments (reasons and claims) from multiple perspectives with a clear explanation of each perspective; thoughtfully analyzes and evaluates all major alternative points of view. |
| 5 | Identifies the salient arguments (reasons and claims) from multiple perspectives; offers analyses and evaluations of most alternative points of view. |
| 4 | Identifies relevant arguments (reasons and claims) from multiple perspectives; offers analyses and evaluations of alternative points of view. |
| 3 | Superficially identifies some arguments (reasons and claims) from main perspectives; superficially evaluates obvious alternative points of view. |
| 2 | Hastily dismisses relevant counterarguments; ignores obvious and important alternative points of view. |
| 1 | Fails to identify relevant counterarguments; ignores all alternative points of view. |

## Reasoning

Reasoning corresponds to CTAR's Inference dimension. It concerns applying or
extending key concepts to draw inferences and analyze implications.

| Score | Descriptor |
|---:|---|
| 6 | Demonstrates confident ability to apply or extend key concepts to make predictions, draw inferences, and analyze implications; demonstrates insightful ability to take concepts further into new territory with broader generalizations. |
| 5 | Demonstrates a clear ability to apply or extend key concepts to make predictions, draw inferences, and analyze implications; demonstrates strong ability to take concepts further with broader generalizations. |
| 4 | Demonstrates an adequate ability to apply or extend key concepts to make predictions, draw inferences, and analyze implications. |
| 3 | Demonstrates an uneven or shaky ability to apply or extend key concepts to make predictions, draw inferences, and analyze implications. |
| 2 | Demonstrates an inadequate ability to apply or extend key concepts to make predictions, draw inferences, and analyze implications. |
| 1 | Demonstrates no ability to apply or extend key concepts to make predictions, draw inferences, and analyze implications. |

## Study scoring and aggregation

Two raters independently scored each dimension for every student turn. The
dimension value used in analysis is the mean of the two rater values. The
turn-level critical-thinking composite is the arithmetic mean of Analysis,
Evaluation, and Reasoning, and therefore remains on the 1-to-6 scale. Dimension
scores and paired rater values are retained separately in the de-identified
numerical table.
